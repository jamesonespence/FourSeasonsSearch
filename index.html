Four Seasons Map v1.18

Four Seasons hotels and resorts

Release notes

v1.18

  • Phone layout: the list sheet now opens at about 40 percent of the screen (drag the handle or tap it for smaller or full height).
  • Weather summary on phones: each property is a card with all 12 months in two rows of six, so nothing scrolls sideways. Each cell shows the month, temperature, season badge and rain bar.

v1.17

  • Simpler left list: just property names (with a small weather chip when the weather bar is on, and marks for favorites, visited and trip stops). Tap a name to open it on the map, where the links and the star, check, trip and weather buttons are.
  • The Weather tab is now a Weather summary button that opens the full screen heatmap directly.
  • New View all button: a full screen list of the properties in view with their Official site, TripAdvisor and Google Maps links.

v1.16

  • Heatmap Overview: the season marker is now a larger badge (dark P, light blue S, white L) and the rain bar is thicker, outlined in white and gets thicker the wetter the month. Cells are taller.

v1.15

  • The Weather tab and its full screen view now show a color key under the lens menu (temperature scale, season letters, rain bars) and a Key button that opens the full explanation.

v1.14

  • New Key button in the weather bar: explains the temperature colors, season rings, rain drops, the overall score, pin shapes and heatmap lenses, with real samples.

v1.13

  • Pins now show three things at once: fill color and number = temperature (daytime high, or daily average with the Temp toggle), ring = tourist season (solid peak, dashed shoulder, none low), blue drop = rain (bigger is wetter). The old single score is still there under Overall score.
  • Goal presets: Beach, Sightseeing, Quiet and pleasant, Ski, or a Custom range. They filter the map, list and heatmap for the month you pick.
  • Good but off-peak now means good weather (score 65+) in shoulder or low tourist season.
  • Heatmap lenses: Overview, Score, Temperature, Rain, Season.
  • Plain-language verdict per month in popups and the weather dialog, for example "mild, mostly dry, shoulder season. Good for sightseeing; not beach weather."
  • Tourist seasons are my own approximations by destination type, not official data, and sea temperature is not included yet.

v1.12

  • Weather tab: the heatmap now lists only the properties in the current map view (same checkbox as the list), and updates as you pan and zoom.
  • Full screen: a Full screen button on the Weather tab opens the heatmap over the whole page, with bigger cells.
  • Weather button (sun icon) on every property in the list and in popups: opens a quick 12-month view with a chart, a month-by-month table of highs, rain, humidity and score, best months, off-peak months and the 7-day forecast.

v1.11

  • Weather: a Weather button on the map opens a month bar. Pick a month (or Now) and every pin turns into a weather score from poor (red) to great (green); the list sorts by it.
  • Good but off-peak: shows properties where the chosen month is nearly as good as their best stretch but outside it.
  • New Weather tab: a heatmap of all properties by month, with best months and off-peak dots. Tap a month to color the map, tap a name to open it.
  • Property popups now show today's weather, a 7-day forecast and a 12-month chart of highs and rain.
  • Units toggle (C or F) and an option to score ski resorts by snow instead of comfort.
  • Data: climate averages from NASA POWER (1991 to 2020), forecast from Open-Meteo. Scores are estimates from daytime highs, rain and humidity, and the climate grid is coarse, so mountains and coastlines can be off.

v1.10

  • TripAdvisor links on phones now try to open the TripAdvisor app first and fall back to the website if the app does not open within a couple of seconds.

v1.9

  • Map labels are now in English everywhere (the previous map showed local languages, for example Chinese place names). Dark mode now uses a true dark map. A Labels toggle in the header (Menu on phones) turns place names off.

v1.8

  • The list now shows only the properties in the current map view, and updates as you pan and zoom. Untick the box above the list to see everything. On phones, the part of the map hidden behind the list is not counted.

v1.7

  • Pins no longer group by default: they just overlap, and the one you hover or tap comes to the front. A Grouping toggle in the header (Menu on phones) brings the numbered clusters back.

v1.6

  • Mobile layout: full-screen map with a pull-up list (drag the handle or tap it to cycle peek, half and full).
  • Filters tucked behind a Filters button, header links behind Menu, compact one-line stats, compact list rows, smaller pins and clusters.
  • The legend is now a collapsible Key button. Tapping a property in the list drops the sheet so you can see the pin and popup.

v1.5

  • Fixed the map background: switched to OpenStreetMap tiles (the previous provider started requiring an API key). Dark mode now darkens the same map.

v1.4

  • Nearby pins now merge into numbered clusters that split as you zoom.
  • Near me button: finds the closest properties and shows distances.
  • Favorites and Visited: tap the star or check on any property. Saved on your device, with a Show filter.
  • Dark mode (Auto, Light or Dark) and a cleaner basemap.
  • Type filter and pin shapes: City hotels (circle), Resorts (rounded square) and Retreats (diamond).
  • Stats strip showing counts by type, favorites and visited.
  • Share button: copies a link that remembers your filters (works on the live site).
  • Trip planner: add properties, reorder them, and see the route and distances on the map.

v1.3

  • New map pins: larger ringed markers with a center dot and shadow that grow on hover, a region color legend with counts, and cleaner labels and popups.

v1.2

  • Version number and release notes added at the top. Credit footer added at the bottom of the list.

v1.1

  • Map now shows a single world: no sideways repeating, and panning is limited to the edges.

v1.0

  • First release: 140 open Four Seasons hotels and resorts on one map, with a searchable list and region filter. Each property links to its official Four Seasons page, TripAdvisor and Google Maps.
